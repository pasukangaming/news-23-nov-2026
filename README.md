A newspaper-style front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026's world news, drawn from scratch with PIL.

Lead story: **Democrats win 258 House seats** — their biggest majority since 1992, a sweep unseen in three decades, with the Senate flipping blue too.

Also covered: a Russian warship hit by suspected Ukrainian sea drones in Benghazi; a candy factory fire in Indonesia with 24 body bags recovered and dozens missing; a Russian drone hitting a building in the Kyiv region, at least 14 missing; two tankers hit by projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz; the FBI probing an apparent breach of its criminal database; a Russian cargo ship still burning off Finland.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the pre-drawn `entry.png` — drawn by `draw_image.py` as a deterministic 1024x1024 PNG — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
